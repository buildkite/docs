# Team management

Manage who can access your Buildkite organization and what they can do. These guides cover user permissions, authentication, and team administration.

- [User and team permissions](/docs/platform/team-management/permissions): Set access to pipelines, test suites, and registries.
- [Enforce 2FA](/docs/platform/team-management/enforce-2fa): Require two-factor authentication for organization members.
- [System banners](/docs/platform/team-management/system-banners): Show organization-wide announcements ([Enterprise](https://buildkite.com/pricing/) plan only).
- [Inactive user list](/docs/platform/team-management/inactive-user-list): Find users who no longer need access.
- [Managing API access tokens](/docs/apis/managing-api-tokens): Control programmatic access to your organization.
